My Story

I am a Nigerian girl who came to America when I was two years old. Growing up between two cultures has shaped who I am today. At home, I was surrounded by Nigerian traditions, food, and values, while outside of home I learned how to adapt to life in America. Because of this, I learned how to balance different parts of my identity and stay open-minded to new experiences.

One thing that describes me best is that I love being busy. I enjoy staying active and always trying something new. Whether it is playing sports, cooking, learning instruments, drawing, or attending educational events, I like challenging myself and learning in different ways. I do not enjoy sitting still for too long because I feel happiest when I am growing, creating, or improving at something.

Sports have taught me discipline and teamwork, especially through activities like girls flag football and powerlifting. Drawing and creative activities allow me to express myself, while cooking helps me connect with my culture and creativity at the same time. I also enjoy educational programs and events because I like learning beyond the classroom and meeting people with different ideas and experiences.

I also hope that one day I will be able to travel the world and experience new cultures, places, and opportunities. I want to continue learning from different people and trying new things because I believe there is always something new to discover.

Overall, I see myself as a curious, hardworking, and creative person. I always want to improve myself, try new opportunities, and continue learning more about both the world and myself.
